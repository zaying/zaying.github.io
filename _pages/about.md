## Biography
I am Wang Jiaying (王佳影), a Ph.D. student at the [Software Institute, Nanjing University](https://software.nju.edu.cn/English/index.html), supervised by [Shouqian Shi](https://shishouqian.cn/). I am part of the team led by [Dean Sheng Zhong](https://www.shengzhong.info/).

Research group: [nju-slab.cn](https://nju-slab.cn/). Email: [wangjiaying@smail.nju.edu.cn](mailto:wangjiaying@smail.nju.edu.cn).

Before that, I received my Master's degree with Distinction in Cyber Security from [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/) in 2021 and did my Master's thesis advised by Dr. [Tasmina Islam](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/tasmina-islam). I received my B.S. in Network Engineering from [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/) in 2020.

I was an EVPN protocol developer and tester at [Huawei](https://www.huawei.com/en/) from 2021 to 2023.

**My research mainly focuses on AI4Health**.

My main research interests are time-series modeling, with a focus on medical EEG, and heart-rate estimation using millimeter-wave radar. I also study trustworthy AI decision-making for complex physiological and biological signals when labels, observations, or reference databases are incomplete, including settings such as metagenomics.
